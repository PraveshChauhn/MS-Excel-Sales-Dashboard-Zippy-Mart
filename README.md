# Zippy Mart — Sales Analysis Dashboard | MS Excel

An interactive **Microsoft Excel Sales Dashboard** built to analyze Zippy Mart's sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends. The project transforms raw sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard using **Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Excel formulas, slicers, and data visualization**.

## Project Overview

Zippy Mart is a retail business that needs a simple and interactive way to monitor its sales performance and identify key business trends.

This project focuses on transforming raw transactional data into meaningful insights that can help management:

* Track overall sales and revenue performance
* Identify top-performing products and categories
* Analyze customer purchasing behavior
* Compare regional sales performance
* Understand sales trends over time
* Identify areas requiring business attention

The final output is an interactive Excel dashboard that allows users to explore the data through dynamic filters and visualizations.

---

## Business Objectives

The dashboard was developed to answer key business questions such as:

1. What is the overall sales and revenue performance?
2. Which products and categories generate the highest sales?
3. Which regions contribute the most to overall sales?
4. How does sales performance change over time?
5. What customer segments contribute significantly to revenue?
6. Which products or regions may require further attention?
7. What trends can be identified from the sales data?
